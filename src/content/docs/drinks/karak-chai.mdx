### Description

A Gulf-style spiced black tea built on a gram-based recipe and one key technique: the foam method. Aerate the tea and milk mixture over heat five times and the milk proteins transform, giving you a velvet texture that sets karak apart from ordinary chai. Two regional variations are included — a Yemeni Shai Adeni and a smoky black cardamom version. Based on the recipe by [Middle Eats](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YxLm0jmazq8).

### Tools

| ------------------ |
| high-walled pot    |
| kitchen scale      |
| fine mesh strainer |

<Tabs syncKey="karak-variation">
  <TabItem label="Gulf Original">

    #### Ingredients

    | Item | Amount |
    | ---- | ------ |
    | water | 355g |
    | evaporated milk | 255g |
    | loose black tea | 8g |
    | white sugar | 25g |
    | fresh ginger, sliced | 3g |
    | cardamom pods, crushed | ~2g (4 pods) |
    | saffron | pinch |
    | cloves | pinch |

    <Aside type="tip">
    The aeration step is what separates karak from regular chai. Each foam-and-settle cycle changes the milk proteins and builds the velvet texture — don't skip reps.
    </Aside>

    #### Recipe

    <Steps>
    1. **Prepare:** Set a high-walled pot on the scale and tare it. Pour in 355g water.

    2. **Add aromatics:** Add the 8g tea, 25g sugar, 3g sliced ginger, and the crushed cardamom, saffron, and cloves.

    3. **Infuse:** Bring to a boil over high heat, then immediately reduce to a simmer. Cook for 5 minutes.

    4. **Add milk:** Pour in 255g evaporated milk and stir to combine.

    5. **Aerate:** Raise to medium-high heat. The moment the foam reaches the rim, pull the pot off the heat and let it settle. Return to heat and repeat — **5 times total**.

    6. **Strain and serve:** Look for a deep terracotta color. Strain into a mug and serve immediately.
    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Shai Adeni">

    #### Ingredients

    | Item | Amount |
    | ---- | ------ |
    | water | 355g |
    | sweetened condensed milk | 40g |
    | loose black tea | 3g |
    | cinnamon stick | ½ stick (~1.5g) |
    | green cardamom pods, crushed | ~2g (4 pods) |
    | whole cloves | 2 pieces |
    | ground nutmeg | pinch |

    #### Recipe

    <Steps>
    1. **Simmer spices:** Add 355g water and all the spices — cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and nutmeg — to the pot. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes until deeply fragrant.

    2. **Steep tea:** Add the 3g tea and steep for 2–4 minutes depending on how strong you like it.

    3. **Add milk:** Stir in 40g sweetened condensed milk and heat through briefly — don't boil.

    4. **Strain and serve:** Strain into a mug and serve. The condensed milk gives this version a thicker, dessert-like body.
    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Black Cardamom">

    #### Ingredients

    | Item | Amount |
    | ---- | ------ |
    | water | 355g |
    | evaporated milk | 255g |
    | loose black tea | 8g |
    | black cardamom pod, lightly crushed | ~1.5g (1 pod) |
    | green cardamom pods, crushed | ~1g (2 pods) |
    | fresh ginger, sliced | 3g |
    | white sugar | 25g |

    <Aside type="tip">
    Black cardamom is a different species from green — it has a smoky, camphor-like quality. Dry-toasting it first draws out the oils before any liquid hits the pan.
    </Aside>

    #### Recipe

    <Steps>
    1. **Toast:** Lightly crush the black cardamom pod and dry-toast it in the cold pot over medium heat for 30 seconds until fragrant.

    2. **Build the base:** Add 355g water, then the 8g tea, 25g sugar, green cardamom, and ginger.

    3. **Infuse:** Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook for 5 minutes.

    4. **Add milk:** Pour in 255g evaporated milk and stir to combine.

    5. **Aerate:** Raise to medium-high heat. Foam to the rim, pull off, let settle — **repeat 5 times**. The smoky oils from the black cardamom emulsify into the milk fats during this process.

    6. **Strain and serve:** Strain into a mug and serve immediately.
    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
