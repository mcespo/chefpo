## Papelón con Limón (Papelón with Lemon ~ Lemonade), Venezuela

### Description

Papelón con Limón is a traditional Venezuelan drink made with papelón (panela) and fresh lemon juice. It's a refreshing beverage, especially popular in hot weather. The recipe is simple and can be adjusted to taste.

### Ingredients

| Item             | Amount                                   |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Papelón/Panela   | 200 - 250 grams (approx. 7-9 oz)         |
| Water            | 1.5 liters (approx. 6 cups)              |
| Fresh Lime Juice | 120 - 150 grams (approx. 1/2 to 3/4 cup) |
| Ice              | For serving                              |

### Tools

| Items |
| ----- |
| pot   |
| stove |

### Recipe

<Steps>
1. Dissolve the Papelón in split amount of water:
   If using a block or cone: Break the papelón into smaller pieces or grate it. Place it in a large pitcher or pot.
   Add about 250-500ml (1-2 cups) of hot or warm water to the papelón. Stir well until the papelón is completely dissolved. This may take 10-30 minutes, stirring occasionally. You can also let it sit in cold water for a longer period (30 minutes to overnight) until dissolved.

2. Add Remaining Water and Lime Juice:
   Once the papelón is fully dissolved, add the remaining cold water to the pitcher.
   Add the freshly squeezed lime juice.

3. Mix and Taste:
   Stir everything together thoroughly.
   Taste and adjust for sweetness or tartness. You can add more papelón (dissolved in a small amount of warm water first) if you prefer it sweeter, or more lime juice if you prefer it tangier.

4. Chill and Serve:
   Refrigerate the Papelón con Limón until thoroughly chilled.
   Serve over plenty of ice in glasses.
</Steps>
