## 4-Ingredient Baked Chicken Meatballs, Yummy Toddler Food

### Description

Based on the recipe by [Yummy Toddler Food](https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/baked-chicken-meatballs/). Four ingredients, one bowl, no stovetop. The carrots are optional but add moisture and a bit of hidden veg.

**Yield:** 16 meatballs (8 servings of 2) | **Total time:** ~35 minutes

### Ingredients

| Item | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ground chicken | 454g (1 lb) | |
| Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs | 65g (⅔ cup) | check weight — varies by brand |
| shredded Parmesan | 50g (½ cup) | |
| egg | x1 | |
| shredded carrots | 55g (½ cup) | optional |

### Tools

| Items |
| --- |
| baking sheet |
| foil |
| cooking spray |
| large mixing bowl |
| tablespoon measure or cookie scoop |

### Recipe

<Steps>
1. **Preheat:** Heat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with foil and coat with cooking spray.

2. **Mix:** Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and mix with clean hands until just combined.

3. **Portion:** Scoop and roll 2-tablespoon portions into balls and place on the prepared baking sheet.

4. **Bake:** Bake for 24–26 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  **Smaller meatballs:** Use 1-tablespoon portions and reduce bake time to 20–22 minutes.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  **Storage:** Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days. Freeze for up to 6 months — thaw overnight in the fridge or reheat in the microwave in 15–30 second increments.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
  **Substitutions:** Gluten-free breadcrumbs work directly. For dairy-free, use vegan Parmesan. For egg-free, omit the egg and add 15g (1 tbsp) of milk instead.
</Aside>
