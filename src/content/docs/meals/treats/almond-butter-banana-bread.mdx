### Description

A rich, cardamom-scented banana bread from [Claire Saffitz](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CilyToUevz0) (*Dessert Person*). Almond butter works both into the batter and as a swirled topping that caramelizes during baking. Best made with very ripe bananas — the darker the better.

**Yield:** 1 loaf (10 slices)

### Ingredients

#### Dry

| Item                              | Amount          |
| --------------------------------- | --------------- |
| all-purpose flour                 | 165g (1⅓ cups) |
| baking powder                     | 4g (1 tsp)      |
| baking soda                       | 3g (½ tsp)      |
| kosher salt                       | 1 tsp           |
| ground cardamom (or cinnamon)     | ¼ tsp           |

#### Wet

| Item                              | Amount              |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| very ripe bananas, mashed         | ~240g (3 bananas)   |
| granulated sugar                  | 100g (½ cup)        |
| large eggs, room temperature      | x2                  |
| smooth almond butter              | 65g (¼ cup)         |
| plain Greek yogurt                | 60g (¼ cup)         |
| coconut oil, melted               | 27g (2 tbsp)        |
| vanilla extract                   | 5g (1 tsp)          |

#### Topping

| Item                  | Amount        |
| --------------------- | ------------- |
| smooth almond butter  | 48g (3 tbsp)  |
| granulated sugar      | 13g (1 tbsp)  |
| coconut oil, melted   | 5g (1 tsp)    |

### Tools

| Items                  |
| ---------------------- |
| 8½"×4½" loaf pan       |
| parchment paper        |
| two large bowls        |
| whisk                  |
| skewer or toothpick    |
| wire rack              |

### Recipe

<Steps>
1. **Prep:** Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a loaf pan with parchment paper and lightly oil.

2. **Make topping:** Stir together 48g almond butter, 13g sugar, and 5g coconut oil until smooth. Set aside.

3. **Mash bananas:** Mash the 3 ripe bananas until smooth — you want about 240g (1 cup).

4. **Mix dry ingredients:** Whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cardamom in a large bowl.

5. **Mix wet ingredients:** In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs and 100g sugar together until lightened and slightly pale. Add the almond butter, yogurt, mashed banana, coconut oil, and vanilla. Whisk until combined.

6. **Combine:** Fold the dry ingredients into the wet batter until just combined — don't overmix.

7. **Fill and swirl:** Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Drop spoonfuls of the topping over the surface, then drag a skewer through in a figure-eight or swirl pattern.

8. **Bake:** Bake for 55 minutes, until deep golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

9. **Cool:** Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Let cool for at least another 15 minutes before slicing.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Store well-wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or freeze individual slices for up to 3 months.
</Aside>
