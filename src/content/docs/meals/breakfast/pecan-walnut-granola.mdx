## Pecan & Walnut Granola, Cookie and Kate

### Description

A crunchy, clumpy granola built around a 200g blend of pecans and walnuts. Precision weighing keeps the oil-to-oat ratio in check, and the cooling time does the real work of creating that satisfying crunch. Based on the recipe by [Cookie and Kate](https://cookieandkate.com/healthy-granola-recipe/).

**Yield:** About 8 cups (16 half-cup servings)

### Ingredients

#### Dry

| Item                            | Amount         |
| ------------------------------- | -------------- |
| old-fashioned rolled oats       | 380g (4 cups)  |
| raw pecans, roughly chopped     | 100g (~¾ cup)  |
| raw walnuts, roughly chopped    | 100g (~⅔ cup)  |
| fine-grain sea salt             | 6g (1 tsp)     |
| ground cinnamon                 | 2g (½ tsp)     |

#### Liquid

| Item                    | Amount         |
| ----------------------- | -------------- |
| coconut oil or olive oil | 115g (½ cup)  |
| maple syrup or honey    | 170g (½ cup)  |
| vanilla extract         | 5g (1 tsp)    |

#### Post-bake add-ins

| Item                          | Amount        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------- |
| dried cranberries             | 90g (⅔ cup)  |
| chocolate chips (optional)    | 90g (½ cup)  |

### Tools

| Items                     |
| ------------------------- |
| large rimmed baking sheet |
| parchment paper           |
| large mixing bowl         |
| spatula                   |

### Recipe

<Steps>
1. **Preheat:** Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. **Mix dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, combine oats, pecans, walnuts, salt, and cinnamon. Stir until evenly distributed.

3. **Add liquids:** Pour in the oil, maple syrup (or honey), and vanilla. Mix thoroughly until every oat is coated.

4. **Spread:** Pour mixture onto the prepared pan and spread into one even layer.

5. **Bake:** Bake for 21–24 minutes. At the halfway point, give it a gentle stir, then press the granola firmly into a flat layer with the back of a spatula — this helps form clusters.

6. **Cool:** Remove from oven when lightly golden. Let cool completely on the pan, undisturbed, for at least 45 minutes. It will feel soft when hot and crisp up as it cools — don't break it apart early.

7. **Finish:** Break into chunks with your hands. Stir in dried cranberries and chocolate chips if using.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Store in an airtight container at room temperature for 1–2 weeks, or freeze in a sealed bag for up to 3 months. Let frozen granola sit at room temperature for 5–10 minutes before serving. In humid conditions, refrigerating helps maintain the crunch.
</Aside>
