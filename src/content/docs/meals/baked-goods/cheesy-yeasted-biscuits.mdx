### Description

Based on the recipe by Claire Saffitz (Dessert Person). Cheesy, laminated yeasted biscuits (angel biscuits) — a hybrid between a flaky buttermilk biscuit and a fluffy dinner roll. The dough is folded with a spreadable seasoned butter instead of a cold butter block, then rests overnight before baking.

The **Protein-Forward** version swaps the buttermilk for blended cottage cheese (acts like buttermilk while adding casein protein) and replaces a portion of the flour with unflavored whey protein isolate. Steamed, finely chopped broccoli folds into the dough alongside the cheddar.

**Yield:** 12 biscuits

### Recipe

<Tabs syncKey="biscuit-version">
  <TabItem label="Original">

    #### Make the Base Dough

    <Steps>
    1. **Combine dry ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk together the 360g of flour, sugar, salt, and instant yeast.

    2. **Cut in butter:** Add the 57g of cold, cubed butter. Use your fingertips to smash the butter into the flour until the largest pieces are lentil-sized.

    3. **Add cheese:** Grate the cheddar directly into the flour mixture on the large holes of a box grater. Toss lightly to coat.

    4. **Mix & hydrate:** Make a well in the center and pour in the 240g of room-temperature buttermilk. Stir with a spatula until a shaggy dough forms. Turn out onto a work surface and gently fold/knead just until all the dry flour is hydrated.

    5. **First proof:** Return the dough to the bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature until expanded by 50–100%, about 45–60 minutes.
    </Steps>

    #### Prepare the Seasoned Butter

    <Steps>
    1. In a small bowl, combine the 113g of softened butter, grated garlic, dill, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Mash with a spatula until smooth and spreadable.
    </Steps>

    #### Laminate

    <Steps>
    1. **Roll out:** Lightly flour your surface and the dough. Roll the dough into a rectangle roughly ⅜-inch thick.

    2. **Spread butter:** Mentally divide the dough into thirds vertically. Dollop and evenly spread the seasoned butter over the bottom two-thirds, going right to the edges. Leave the top third plain.

    3. **Fold 1:** Fold the plain top third down over the middle third, then fold the bottom third up over the center, like a letter.

    4. **Fold 2:** Turn the packet 90° so the open seam is on your right. Roll out again to ⅜-inch thick, then do another letter fold.

    5. **Chill:** Wrap in plastic. Freeze for 10 minutes, then refrigerate for 30 minutes to relax the gluten and keep the butter cold.

    6. **Fold 3:** Lightly flour the surface. Position the seam to the right and roll out one last time into a rectangle. Do a final letter fold.

    7. **Overnight rest:** Wrap tightly and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, up to 16 hours max.
    </Steps>

    #### Cut, Proof, and Bake (Next Day)

    <Steps>
    1. **Trim & cut:** Place the cold dough on a cutting board. Trim the rough outer edges slightly to expose the laminated layers. Cut into a 3x4 grid for 12 square biscuits — use a sharp knife and straight up-and-down motions.

    2. **Second proof:** Transfer the squares to a parchment-lined sheet tray. Cover loosely and let rise at cool room temperature until slightly domed and puffy, about 1–1½ hours.

    3. **Bake:** Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Brush the tops with melted butter. Bake on the center rack for 15–20 minutes, until deeply golden brown.

    4. **Cool & serve:** Transfer to a wire rack, let cool slightly, and serve warm.
    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Protein-Forward">

    #### Prep the Broccoli & Cottage Cheese

    <Steps>
    1. **Broccoli:** Steam the broccoli florets until tender-crisp. Cool completely, chop into tiny pieces (small confetti, not chunks), and press between paper towels to remove excess moisture.

    2. **Cottage cheese base:** Blend the 200g of cottage cheese with ~40g of milk in a small blender until completely smooth. Bring to room temperature before using.
    </Steps>

    #### Mix the Dough

    <Steps>
    1. **Combine dry ingredients:** Whisk the 240g of flour, whey protein isolate, sugar, salt, and instant yeast in a large bowl.

    2. **Cut in butter:** Add the 42g of cold, cubed butter and work it into the flour with your fingertips until broken down into lentil-sized bits.

    3. **Add broccoli & cheddar:** Toss the grated cheddar and chopped broccoli into the flour mixture.

    4. **Hydrate:** Pour in the blended cottage cheese mixture. Stir with a spatula until a shaggy dough comes together, then gently knead on the counter just until no dry flour spots remain.

    5. **First proof:** Cover in the bowl and let rise at room temperature for 45–60 minutes, until puffed by ~50%.
    </Steps>

    #### Laminate & Chill

    <Steps>
    1. **Garlic-herb butter:** Mix the 85g of softened butter with the garlic, dill, onion powder, salt, and pepper.

    2. **Roll & spread:** Roll the dough into a ⅜-inch thick rectangle. Spread the garlic butter over the bottom two-thirds. Letter fold: top third down, bottom third up.

    3. **Second turn:** Rotate 90° (seam to the right), roll out to ⅜-inch, and fold in thirds again.

    4. **Chill:** Freeze 10 minutes, then refrigerate 30 minutes.

    5. **Final turn & long rest:** Roll, fold a 3rd time, wrap, and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight.
    </Steps>

    #### Cut, Proof, and Bake (Next Day)

    <Steps>
    1. **Cut:** Trim the outer edges and slice the dough into 12 squares (3x4 grid).

    2. **Second proof:** Place on a parchment-lined pan, cover, and let rest at cool room temperature for 1 hour, until slightly domed.

    3. **Bake:** Preheat the oven to 385°F (195°C) — slightly lower than the original to keep the protein isolate from over-browning. Brush the tops with melted butter and bake for 16–20 minutes until golden brown.
    </Steps>

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="tip">
  **Keys to clean layers and a good rise:** Keep the butter cool while folding, don't over-fold (3 letter folds total is the sweet spot), and always cut the dough cold with a sharp knife using straight up-and-down motions.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
  **Why this method works:**
  - **Yeast + buttermilk** combines the tender, flaky layers of a biscuit with the fluffy, airy crumb of a dinner roll.
  - **Spreadable seasoned butter** is easier than a cold butter block and puts the garlic and herbs right between the layers.
  - **Square cuts** mean no scraps and no re-rolling, so the edge lamination stays pristine.
</Aside>

### Original vs. Protein-Forward

| Element        | Original                                  | Protein-Forward                                      | Why                                                                                    |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| protein source | buttermilk & cheese (~8g protein/biscuit) | cottage cheese + whey isolate (~11g protein/biscuit) | more protein and fewer calories while keeping the structure                            |
| hydration base | buttermilk                                | blended cottage cheese + milk                        | replicates buttermilk's acidity and moisture, with lots of casein                      |
| add-ins        | cheddar only                              | cheddar + chopped broccoli                           | adds color, texture, and micronutrients; pat the broccoli dry so water doesn't ruin the layers |
| bake temp      | 400°F (200°C)                             | 385°F (195°C)                                        | whey browns faster; the lower temp keeps the tops from burning before the center cooks |
